# Sales Insights — FMCG Industry 📊

## Project Overview
Analyzed 1,000+ FMCG sales transactions to identify 
revenue trends, top customers, and regional performance 
using Excel and SQL.

## Tools Used
- Excel (VLOOKUP, Pivot Tables, Charts, Data Cleaning)
- MySQL (Joins, Group By, Aggregations)
- Power BI (In Progress)

## Data Cleaning Steps
- Removed 9 invalid rows (negative & zero sales)
- Converted 4 USD transactions to INR (×83)
- Merged 5 tables using VLOOKUP
- Handled hidden characters using TRIM & CLEAN functions

## Key Business Insights
1. Delhi NCR is the highest revenue generating market
2. 2018 was the peak revenue year
3. Electricalsara Stores is the top customer
4. North zone contributes highest revenue (51%)
5. All transactions were Own Brand products

## SQL Queries Covered
- Total Revenue & Sales Quantity
- Revenue by Market (JOIN + GROUP BY)
- Top 5 Customers by Revenue
- Year & Month wise Revenue Trends
- USD to INR currency filtering
